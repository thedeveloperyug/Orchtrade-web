General information only

OrchTrade is an independent trading software, analytics and research platform. Its charts, portfolio information, AI-generated research, watch-only signals and backtests are informational and educational.

OrchTrade does not provide personalized investment advice, place orders or guarantee investment outcomes.

Financial market and leverage risks

Foreign exchange, commodities, futures, derivatives and leveraged trading involve material financial risk. Prices can change rapidly, and leverage magnifies losses. Depending on your broker, instrument and applicable protections, you may lose more than your original deposit. Understand the risks and your own financial circumstances before trading.

AI-generated insights

AI outputs may be inaccurate, misleading, incomplete or based on outdated information. A BUY, SELL or WAIT label is a watch-only research classification, not a recommendation to trade. Historical win rates, confidence scores and risk/reward scenarios are not validated forecasts of future profitable trades. Independently verify any analysis before acting.

Signal and trade-setup limitations

Signals and research scenarios can become invalid when markets move, spreads change, events occur or broker data is delayed. OrchTrade makes no promise about timing, reliability or profitability and cannot execute a trade for you.

Backtesting is hypothetical

Historical simulations depend on the available dataset, chosen rules, fees, spread, slippage, broker execution assumptions and market conditions. They do not fully reproduce real trading. Past performance and simulated results do not guarantee future outcomes.

Market-data accuracy and delay

Quotes, account metrics, economic calendars and historical candles may come from brokers or third-party sources. They can be stale, unavailable or incorrect. Confirm any time-sensitive information at the original source; OrchTrade does not guarantee zero-latency pricing.

Independent broker services

OrchTrade is not a broker, custodian or fund manager. Your broker controls trading conditions, orders, spreads, liquidation, withdrawals and account access. Integrating with a broker does not mean OrchTrade endorses it or verifies its regulatory status.

Jurisdictional restrictions

Trading rules differ by country. Residents of India must comply with applicable Reserve Bank of India and foreign-exchange laws. Seeing or connecting to a financial instrument or broker through OrchTrade does not make that trading activity lawful.

No guaranteed returns

OrchTrade does not guarantee profits, loss recovery, trade win rates, risk-free setups or protection against drawdowns. Example values displayed on its public website are illustrative rather than live trading accounts or expected performance.

Your responsibility

All trading decisions remain yours. If appropriate, consult a qualified, properly authorized financial professional. This notice supplements the Terms & Conditions and does not remove any statutory rights or remedies.
