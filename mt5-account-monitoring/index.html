MT5 Account Monitoring & Portfolio Tracker

OrchTrade is a web-based MT5 account monitoring and portfolio analytics platform. It reads supported broker account data and brings open trades, floating profit and loss, equity and trading history into one dashboard without executing orders.

What can an MT5 portfolio tracker show?

Balance represents account funds after realized transactions; equity also reflects open-position P&L. Floating P&L changes while positions remain open, whereas realized P&L comes from completed trades. Review these separately rather than treating floating gains as banked profit. OrchTrade brings these metrics and account history together when supplied by the connected broker.

How to connect a supported MT5 account

Open the OrchTrade dashboard, sign in and follow the account-connection workflow. Select your broker server and use authorized MT5 investor/read-only credentials where supported. Check that the broker name, account and currency are correct, then wait for the first successful sync. Never enter master trading passwords into journal entries or AI prompts.

What to check when account data looks delayed

Confirm that the market is open, that your broker is publishing ticks and that the connection status is healthy. Compare the most recent account figures with your broker terminal. Disconnects, market closures and broker feeds can affect freshness. OrchTrade does not promise zero-latency updates.

Which plan supports multiple MT5 accounts?

Free supports one connected MT5 account. Pro and Advanced support up to two. Both paid tiers list ultra-low-latency sync and priority customer care; actual data speed depends on connectivity and service availability. The dashboard does not place or modify orders.
