What is OrchTrade? +

OrchTrade is a web-based trading analytics and research platform for supported MT5 accounts. It combines account monitoring, trading journals, economic news and plan-dependent AI insights, backtesting and charts. It is not a broker and does not execute trades.

How much does OrchTrade cost? +

Free is $0, Pro is $12 per month and Advanced is $25 per month at current USD offer prices. Yearly prepaid access costs $144 and $300 respectively. Check available currency, taxes and final price in the dashboard.

Does OrchTrade place trades for me? +

No. OrchTrade is designed as a read-only monitoring and research platform. AI trade setups are watch-only research, not orders or guaranteed outcomes.

Can I connect a MetaTrader 5 account? +

Free supports 1 connected MT5 account; Pro and Advanced support up to 2 each. Where available, use the broker's investor/read-only credentials. Connection availability depends on your broker and deployment.

Is AI included in the Free plan? +

Yes. Free includes a 1-day AI Insights trial and a 1-day Backtests trial. Pro includes AI Insights throughout the plan and a 3-day Backtests trial. Advanced includes Backtests, AI Insights, Market Charts, Signals and additional AI research tools.

How do I choose a plan? +

Start with Free for $0, or choose Pro or Advanced and sign in to review that plan in your dashboard. Paid plans renew manually when access ends.

Are the numbers on this website live? +

No. The example dashboard is a design illustration with sample data. Your actual dashboard uses available data from your connected account.
