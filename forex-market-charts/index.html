Where do OrchTrade market prices come from?

Market data is supplied through supported connected MT5 accounts. Instruments, symbol names, pricing, available history and trading sessions vary by broker. A symbol that your broker does not offer may not be available in the workspace.

How to use multiple charts

Choose supported symbols and timeframes to compare different markets or perspectives. The workspace supports multiple charts and a full-window view for closer inspection. Confirm the current candle's timestamp and price against the broker when information is time-sensitive.

What does AI scan do?

The Advanced plan includes real-time charts with AI scan and trade setup research. A scan may summarize a chart or present conditional entry, stop and risk-reward levels. It is watch-only and does not create or modify orders. No signal guarantees market direction.

How fast are market updates?

The platform is designed for timely synchronization; Pro and Advanced list ultra-low-latency sync. Actual speed depends on available broker ticks, market sessions, infrastructure and internet connectivity. No fixed millisecond latency is guaranteed. Market Charts are listed in Advanced, not Free or Pro.
