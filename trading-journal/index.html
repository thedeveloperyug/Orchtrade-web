MT5 Trading Journal & Trade Review

An MT5 trading journal connects trade records with your own notes about decisions, execution and outcomes. OrchTrade provides a journal for reviewing connected account history and documenting your process, rather than placing new trades.

What should a trading journal contain?

Record the instrument, direction, entry and exit, initial trade thesis, planned stop and target, and costs if available. Add what actually happened, whether you followed your plan and what you would do differently. Keep trade outcome separate from decision quality.

A practical review workflow

Select a completed or open trade from the journal. Write your pre-trade reasoning and post-trade review, tag the setup and record emotions or execution mistakes. Use an execution checklist and process rating to track whether you followed your rules. Avoid using outcome alone as a measure of good decisions.

How to review a trading week

Group completed trades by setup and instrument. Compare win rate alongside average win, average loss and costs; a high win rate can still coincide with negative net results. Check repeated violations such as late entries, missing risk controls or unplanned exits. Choose one measurable process improvement for the next review.

Does OrchTrade have a free journal?

Yes. Journal access is listed in Free, Pro and Advanced. The Free plan supports one MT5 account. Advanced adds the AI Journal Coach, which can help with research-oriented review; its suggestions can be incomplete or mistaken. You remain responsible for the interpretation.
