AI Trading Analysis & Portfolio Insights

OrchTrade offers AI-assisted trading research based on available account and market data. It helps users review portfolio metrics, explore strategy rules and examine watch-only trade scenarios; it does not execute trades or provide guaranteed predictions.

What can AI portfolio analysis help review?

Start with observable metrics such as realized and unrealized P&L, win rate, average win and loss, drawdown and open exposure. An AI explanation can help identify questions to investigate, but should be checked against the underlying account records. A model cannot reliably infer every cause of a trading outcome.

What does a watch-only trading signal mean?

A BUY, SELL or WAIT classification describes a research scenario. It does not instruct OrchTrade to open a position. An illustrated entry, stop or risk-reward target is conditional on the scenario and can become invalid as prices change. Independently verify data before deciding whether to trade.

Which OrchTrade plan includes AI?

Free includes a one-day AI Insights trial. Pro includes AI Insights throughout the paid plan. Advanced includes AI Insights, AI Backtesting & Strategy Assistant, AI Signals (watch-only), AI Trade Scan / Setup Analysis and AI Journal Coach. Features are subject to available data and service configuration.

What are the risks of relying on AI?

AI outputs can be inaccurate, stale or misleading. They may misread a pattern, omit transaction costs or produce unjustified confidence. Treat them as one source of research, not personalized investment advice. Do not equate model confidence with a validated probability of profit.
