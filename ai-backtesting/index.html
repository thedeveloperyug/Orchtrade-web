What is strategy backtesting?

Backtesting applies specified entry, exit and risk rules to historical prices. A meaningful simulation defines which candles are available at each decision point, how orders are modeled, and how costs are handled. It measures hypothetical historical outcomes, not future profitability.

How to prepare a test

Choose an instrument such as XAUUSD or a currency pair, a timeframe and date range. Check candle coverage before running a test. Import appropriate historical CSV data or use available stored data. Specify precise entry and exit conditions, stop logic, position sizing and cost assumptions. Review how the strategy handles missing candles and market closures.

How to evaluate the results

Inspect simulated trades with replay, review drawdowns and examine whether results depend on a small number of wins. Use separate training and holdout periods where available and avoid tuning a strategy repeatedly to the same unseen sample. Historical prices and modeled fills may not match live broker execution.

Is AI backtesting free?

Free includes a one-day Backtests trial; Pro includes a three-day trial. Advanced includes Backtests throughout the paid access period and the AI Backtesting & Strategy Assistant. AI can help express and review rules, but must not be treated as proof that a strategy is correct or profitable.
